Image/ISO Inspector

Client-side ISO/IMG browser. Supports: ISO, IMG (MBR), and wrappers: zip, tar, tgz/tar.gz, gz, xz. FAT partitions supported (stuff like bootfs). No RAR. No writing to disks.

Notes / troubleshooting
  • If your file is .rar, convert it to .zip or .xz first.
  • For huge images, use a desktop tool if the browser runs out of memory.
  • For .img, this app currently parses MBR partitions. GPT images may not show partitions.
